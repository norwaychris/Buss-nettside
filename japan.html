The Japan Story

Ten days. Four cities. One mission: fill your crates with things you cannot buy anywhere else on Earth.

Episode 01 · Day 1 · Tokyo

The trip begins

Two suitcases, one empty duffel bag, and a list of shops gathered from collectors over three years. We landed in Tokyo at dusk and went straight to Akihabara — no hotel check-in, no dinner. The hunt started immediately.

Episode 02 · Day 2 · Shibuya

Pokémon Center

The Shibuya Pokémon Center is a temple. Store-exclusive promo cards, collaboration items that sell out in hours, sleeves and boxes that never leave Japan. We queued before opening — twice — to secure exclusives for the crates.

Episode 03 · Day 4 · Nakano

The hidden stores

Nakano Broadway doesn't look like much from the street. Inside: four floors of glass cases holding decades of Japanese card history. We spent nine hours here and left with promos we had only ever seen in photographs.

Episode 04 · Day 6 · Kyoto

Vintage territory

Kyoto's backstreet shops are where the old cards live. Base Set era Japanese holos, No Rarity prints, cards that predate the global boom. The owners don't advertise. You have to know where to knock — and bow, and be patient.

Episode 05 · Day 8 · Osaka

Buying the products

Den Den Town, Osaka. This is where the sealed product came from — Japanese booster boxes bought over the counter, verified on the spot, wrapped and carried by hand. No middlemen, no distributors. Us, the shop, and the boxes.

Episode 06 · Day 10 · Oslo

Packing the mystery crates

Back home, every item was laid out, photographed and catalogued. Then the crates were packed by hand — each one balanced so that every single crate carries something we would be excited to open ourselves. That's the rule.

The crates are ready

Everything we found in Japan, sealed and waiting. Limited quantities — once they're gone, they're gone.
